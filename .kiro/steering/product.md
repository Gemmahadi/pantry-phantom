# Product — Pantry Phantom

## What it is

Pantry Phantom answers: **"What can I cook tonight with the food I already have?"**

A home cook opens the app, taps the ingredients already in their kitchen, and
immediately sees which of 24 real recipes they can cook — with a match
percentage and the exact missing ingredients for each one. Fully cookable
recipes glow green. Missing items go to a shopping list in one tap.

## Who it is for

- Home cooks who hate wasting food and hate extra shopping trips.
- Anyone staring into a half-full fridge at 6pm wondering what dinner is.
- Halloween lovers (Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest): every recipe wears a
  spooky costume — Witch's Brew Pumpkin Soup, Mummy Dogs, Black Cat Brownies —
  but the recipes underneath are real and cookable.

## Goals

1. **Useful first, spooky second.** The Halloween theme must never get in the
   way of finding dinner. If a design choice trades usability for theme, usability wins.
2. **Zero friction.** No account, no sign-up, no tracking, no ads. Open the page
   and cook.
3. **Honest matching.** The match % is a plain count of ingredients on hand —
   no hidden scoring, no sponsored recipes.
4. **Private by design.** Pantry, favourites, and shopping list live only in the
   user's browser (`localStorage`). Nothing is sent anywhere.

## Non-goals

- No user accounts, cloud sync, or social features.
- No AI-generated recipes — all 24 recipes are hand-written and real.
- No backend or database. The app is static files.
- No monetisation in the hackathon build.
